Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

AdGuard Budget Monitor collects the following information when you connect your advertising accounts:

  • Ad account identifiers and names
  • Campaign spend and budget data
  • Campaign performance metrics (impressions, clicks, conversions)
  • Your email address for account creation and notifications

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Display your ad spend data in the dashboard
  • Send budget alerts and notifications
  • Automatically pause campaigns when thresholds are exceeded (if enabled)
  • Improve our services

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Access tokens are encrypted and stored securely. We do not sell or share your advertising data with third parties.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can disconnect your ad accounts at any time, which will revoke our access to your advertising data.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us at: privacy@adguard-monitor.com
